Who We Are

Our Story

Founded on Passion

StrengthCloud started with a simple frustration: Indian gamers and developers were paying too much for too little. We wanted to change that by building infrastructure that punches way above its price.

Our founders are gamers first and business people second. Every decision we make — from hardware to support — comes from a player's perspective, not a spreadsheet.

Today, we host Minecraft servers, VPS instances, Discord bots, and more for a growing community of builders and players across India and beyond.

Built for Real Performance

We run on enterprise-grade NVMe SSD hardware in premium Mumbai data centers, giving you the lowest possible latency from within India. No shared spinning drives, no overselling.

Every plan comes with DDoS protection built in — not as a paid add-on. Because we know how frustrating it is to get taken offline mid-game.

Our Pterodactyl-powered game panel gives you instant server control, live console access, file management, and automated backups — all in one clean interface.
